## 3. Key Policy Interpretation

### 3.1 Account access and transfer

TikTok Terms state that users must keep passwords confidential, are responsible for activity under their account, and must not use another person's account without TikTok authorization. The Terms also describe the access/use license as non-transferable and prohibit transferring/selling the Services.

Business implication:

- Selling an existing TikTok account as a login/asset is not a clean compliant product.
- Tech must not build features that normalize credential storage, credential resale, cookie transfer, or automated takeover.
- Sales must not promise "account transfer is TikTok-approved" unless there is written authorization or official process from TikTok.

### 3.2 Fake engagement and deceptive behavior

TikTok Community Guidelines prohibit deceptive account behavior, fake engagement, selling followers/likes, bots, scripts, fake reviews, and manipulating recommendation systems.

Business implication:

- Any sourced channel must not have fake follower/fake engagement sourcing.
- No product copy should say "tang follow", "buff tuong tac", "tool nuoi", "bao view".
- Audit should include follower/engagement quality signals.
- Channels suspected of fake engagement must be rejected or marked high risk.

### 3.3 Circumvention

TikTok prohibits using another account to bypass bans/restrictions or continue restricted behavior.

Business implication:

- Do not sell channels to customers who disclose that their old account was restricted/banned and they want a replacement to continue the same behavior.
- Add a lead/order compliance checkbox: "Customer confirms this is not being purchased to bypass an existing TikTok restriction or ban."
- If customer intends to bypass feature restriction, reject or flag for founder review.

### 3.4 LIVE and off-platform selling

TikTok LIVE policy says commercial LIVE content should be disclosed. It also states TikTok may reduce visibility of LIVE content that directs users to buy products off-platform in markets where TikTok Shop is available. Such LIVE sessions can be FYF ineligible.

Business implication:

- Do not market the product as "kenh de keo khach mua ngoai TikTok Shop".
- If customer uses LIVE for commerce, recommended compliant path is TikTok Shop where applicable, or lead generation/consultation wording rather than direct off-platform purchase pushes.
- Sales should not promise LIVE reach if customer's plan is direct off-platform purchase.
- Playbooks must avoid instructions like "cach ne TikTok Shop" or "cach noi lach de keo mua ngoai".

### 3.5 External links, landing pages and lead generation

TikTok Ads policies allow landing pages and lead generation flows, but require functional pages, privacy policy, visible business/contact information, and no sensitive/prohibited data collection. TikTok Destination Links exist for eligible Verified Business Accounts and selected regions/use cases.

Business implication:

- External link/landing page use is not automatically prohibited, but it must be clean, functional and transparent.
- Landing pages should include company name, contact details, policies, terms, privacy policy and lawful product information.
- Lead forms should not request sensitive data such as government ID, financial data, health data, passwords, or exact age unless explicitly allowed and legally compliant.
- For ads, ensure ad creative and landing page are consistent.

### 3.6 Commercial disclosure

When content promotes a brand/product/service, TikTok requires commercial content disclosure where applicable. TikTok says undisclosed commercial content may lose For You distribution eligibility.

Business implication:

- Editor/content pack should include disclosure guidance.
- For paid collaborations or brand promotion, instruct customer to use the commercial content disclosure setting where required.
- Do not recommend hiding commercial intent to improve reach.

### 3.7 TikTok Shop official account

TikTok Seller Center describes an official account as an account that officially represents a verified business/shop. Linking official account can provide e-commerce video and LIVE permissions.

Business implication:

- For customers who sell products in TikTok Shop markets, the cleanest route is helping them set up their own Seller Center and official account.
- Product roadmap should include "client-owned account setup" as compliant alternative to transferred account inventory.

## 4. Risk Classification For Team

### Green Zone - Build and sell

These are safer, policy-aligned products:

- Content starter pack.
- Video editing package.
- Script/hook/caption/CTA pack.
- TikTok launch playbook.
- TikTok Shop/Seller Center setup support.
- Client-owned Business Account setup.
- Landing page/link bio setup with privacy policy.
- Lead form setup with proper privacy policy.
- Audit/reporting of a customer's own account.
- Sales/lead workflow outside TikTok, as long as ads/landing/privacy/product rules are followed.

### Yellow Zone - Allowed only with controls

These need disclosure, rules and review:

- Public catalog of "social/channel assets" without showing full handle.
- Audit and brokerage-like workflow around existing channels.
- Customers using external landing/Zalo for consultation or lead generation.
- LIVE scripts that mention consultation/contact, not direct off-platform purchase pressure.
- Branded/commercial content, if disclosure is used properly.

Controls:

- Compliance checklist.
- Risk disclosure.
- No absolute guarantee.
- Reject restricted/prohibited categories.
- Review landing pages and product claims.

### Red Zone - Do not build or promote

These should not be productized:

- Selling "acc unlock live/link/shop".
- Selling followers, likes, comments, fake engagement.
- Bot/tool-based account farming.
- Storing TikTok passwords, cookies, session tokens or 2FA backup codes.
- Helping customers bypass bans, restrictions or LIVE suspensions.
- "Cach noi laich/cach ne policy" scripts.
- Direct off-platform purchase push during LIVE in TikTok Shop markets.
- Prohibited or restricted products without legal/platform qualification.
- Misleading claims, fake reviews, fake identity, impersonation.

## 5. System Requirements From Policy

### 5.1 Must-have controls

- Add compliance status to each channel/order: `unchecked`, `passed`, `high_risk`, `rejected`.
- Add channel risk level: `low`, `medium`, `high`, `rejected`.
- Add customer intended-use field.
- Add restricted-use flags:
  - bypass_ban_or_restriction
  - direct_off_platform_purchase_live
  - prohibited_or_sensitive_product
  - fake_engagement_suspected
  - credential_transfer_required
- Add mandatory risk disclosure acceptance before order can move to payment/handover.
- Add no-guarantee acknowledgement:
  - no guarantee of reach
  - no guarantee of LIVE
  - no guarantee of link bio
  - no guarantee of TikTok Shop
  - no guarantee of ads approval
  - no guarantee against platform enforcement
- Add audit log for every status change.
- Add block rule: rejected/high-risk compliance cannot be publicly listed without admin override.

### 5.2 Credential safety

System must not store:

- TikTok password.
- Email password.
- 2FA code.
- Cookie/session token.
- Backup code.
- Personal identity documents unless legally needed and properly protected.

Allowed:

- Handover checklist status.
- Evidence screenshot/video without exposing secrets.
- Non-sensitive notes.
- Customer acknowledgement logs.

### 5.3 Lead and order compliance questions

Add to lead/order form:

- "San pham/nganh cua anh chi la gi?"
- "Muc dich su dung kenh?"
- "Anh chi co dang bi TikTok han che/khoa tinh nang tai khoan cu khong?"
- "Anh chi co dinh dung kenh de tiep tuc mot hanh vi dang bi han che tren tai khoan khac khong?"
- "Anh chi co ban hang truc tiep qua TikTok Shop hay chi dung de tu van/lead?"
- "Anh chi co can landing page/privacy policy khong?"

If customer indicates bypass/restriction behavior, system should flag the order for admin review or rejection.

## 7. Recommended Product Pivot For Highest Compliance

If the company wants the cleanest long-term path, prioritize:

```text
Client-owned TikTok Business/TikTok Shop setup
+ Content starter pack
+ Landing/lead setup
+ Account audit
+ Optional sourcing advisory
```

If the company continues channel inventory resale, classify it internally as high-risk and do not present it as fully TikTok-compliant.

Recommended product names:

- TikTok Launch Pack
- TikTok Content Starter Pack
- TikTok Lead Funnel Pack
- TikTok Shop/Business Setup Pack
- Audited Channel Advisory

Avoid product names:

- Acc unlock pack
- Bao live account
- Acc keo Zalo
- Acc full tinh nang
