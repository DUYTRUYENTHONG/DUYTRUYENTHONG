# BRD - TikTok Channel Starter Marketplace

Version: 0.1  
Date: 2026-06-04  
Owner: Founder / Product Owner  
Build direction: Inventory-first marketplace, manual-first operations, audit-driven trust layer  
Related system: Growth Commerce OS  
Compliance memo: `TikTok_Policy_Compliance_Memo.md`

## 1. Executive Summary

TikTok Channel Starter Marketplace la san pham giup shop/doanh nghiep bat dau nhanh tren TikTok bang cach mua hoac nhan ban giao cac kenh TikTok nho da co tuong tac san, duoc audit theo tieu chuan noi bo, va co the mua kem content starter pack do doi editor san xuat.

Trong giai doan hien tai, cong ty khong muon tiep tuc mo rong theo mo hinh agency full-service vi ton nhieu cong van hanh, support va follow khach. Huong chien luoc moi la productize dich vu thanh:

- Kho kenh TikTok da audit.
- Bo tieu chuan cham diem rui ro va chat luong kenh.
- Quy trinh giu cho / dat coc / ban giao / bao hanh gioi han.
- Content starter pack co scope co dinh, ban kem nhu add-on.
- Landing page/public catalog de khach xem hang va de lai nhu cau.
- He thong noi bo de quan ly nguon hang, audit, don hang, ban giao, editor pack va warranty.

Muc tieu la chuyen doanh thu tu "lam dich vu met" sang "ban tai san truyen thong + add-on co dong goi", giup scale tot hon, bien loi nhuan ro hon va giam phu thuoc vao founder.

## 2. Business Context

Shop/doanh nghiep nho tai Viet Nam dang can bat dau tren TikTok nhanh de keo traffic, live, lam noi dung va day khach ve kenh chot rieng nhu Zalo, Messenger, landing page hoac link bio. Nhieu khach khong muon xay kenh tu con so 0 vi ton thoi gian, chua du tinh nang, hoac thieu trust ban dau.

Cong ty hien co loi the:

- Co kha nang thu mua/tao nguon kenh nho 5k-10k follow.
- Hieu nhu cau thuc te cua shop khi can kenh co tuong tac san.
- Co doi editor co the san xuat video ngan theo goi.
- Co san nen tang website/landing/CMS de tao public page, form lead, sales portfolio va workflow noi bo.

Van de hien tai:

- Neu tiep tuc ban theo cach "nhan dich vu van hanh", founder va team de bi qua tai.
- Neu truyen thong la "ban acc/unlock tinh nang", rui ro hinh anh va policy cao.
- Neu khong co audit/report/bang ton kho ro rang, khach se xem nhu hang troi noi va ep gia.
- Neu khong co quy trinh ban giao/bao hanh, de phat sinh tranh chap sau ban.

## 3. Market And Growth Thesis

### 3.1 Market Signal

Thi truong TikTok/social commerce tai Viet Nam van co growth tot:

- DataReportal Digital 2026 Vietnam ghi nhan TikTok co khoang 76.1 trieu nguoi dung 18+ tai Viet Nam theo TikTok advertising resources.
- e-Conomy SEA 2025 uoc tinh kinh te so Viet Nam dat khoang 39 ty USD trong nam 2025, tiep tuc tang truong hai chu so.
- Cac shop nho ngay cang dung TikTok nhu kenh discovery, lead generation, live commerce va social proof.

Reference sources:

- DataReportal Digital 2026 Vietnam: https://datareportal.com/reports/digital-2026-vietnam
- Google/Temasek/Bain e-Conomy SEA 2025 Vietnam report: https://services.google.com/fh/files/misc/vietnam_e_conomy_sea_2025_report.pdf
- TikTok Terms of Service: https://m.tiktok.com/legal/page/us/terms-of-service/vi
- TikTok Community Guidelines: https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features

### 3.2 Strategic Thesis

Thay vi ban dich vu "lam thay khach", cong ty nen ban "starting advantage":

```text
Audited TikTok Channel + Content Starter Pack + Handover Playbook
= faster start for shops that want to self-operate.
```

Gia tri cot loi khong nam o follower count don thuan. Gia tri nam o:

- Kenh duoc loc rui ro.
- Co lich su tuong tac that hon hang troi noi.
- Co thong tin minh bach ve niche, view, comment, rui ro.
- Co bo content/kich ban giup khach bat dau dang ngay.
- Co quy trinh ban giao va bao hanh gioi han ro rang.

### 3.3 Product-Market Fit Hypothesis

Khach hang phu hop nhat:

- Shop nho/moi, da co san pham nhung chua co kenh TikTok tot.
- Shop dang ban qua Zalo/Facebook, muon them nguon traffic TikTok.
- Doanh nghiep dia phuong can kenh de live/lam noi dung ban don ngoai.
- Seller affiliate/review san pham can kenh co tuong tac ban dau.
- Agency nho/SMM freelancer can mua kenh cho khach cua ho.

Khach hang khong phu hop:

- Brand lon can compliance cao va hop dong dai han.
- Nganh cam/nhay cam theo chinh sach TikTok hoac phap luat.
- Khach muon cam ket don hang, cam ket live vinh vien, cam ket reach.
- Khach can full-service van hanh hang thang.

## 4. Product Vision

Xay dung he thong "Social Asset Marketplace" noi bo va public-facing, bat dau voi TikTok channel inventory, sau do co the mo rong sang:

- Facebook page/group.
- Instagram account.
- YouTube Shorts channel.
- Landing/bio page asset.
- Content template pack.
- Editor production pack.

Core product model:

```text
Source Channel -> Audit -> List -> Reserve -> Sell -> Handover -> Optional Content Pack -> Limited Warranty
```

He thong phai giup team tra loi 5 cau hoi:

1. Hien co bao nhieu kenh san sang ban?
2. Kenh nao sach, kenh nao rui ro?
3. Kenh nao phu hop voi nganh cua khach?
4. Don nao dang giu cho/thanh toan/ban giao?
5. Editor pack nao dang trong pipeline va loi nhuan tung don la bao nhieu?

## 7. Non-Goals For MVP

MVP khong build:

- Marketplace public cho seller ben ngoai dang hang truc tiep.
- Auto login, luu password, luu cookie, hoac bypass 2FA cua social accounts.
- Auto transfer account bang tool khong chinh thuc.
- Auto verify tinh nang TikTok bang scraping nang.
- CRM phuc tap voi deal pipeline nhieu cap.
- Payment gateway tich hop ngay tu dau.
- Cam ket reach, don hang, live, link bio, ads, TikTok Shop.
- He thong van hanh full-service content/live hang thang.

## 8. Compliance And Policy Position

Viec mua ban/chuyen giao tai khoan social co rui ro theo dieu khoan nen tang. He thong can duoc thiet ke de quan ly rui ro, minh bach thong tin va tranh khuyen khich hanh vi vi pham.

Important: team must read `TikTok_Policy_Compliance_Memo.md` before implementing inventory, sales, public copy, handover or warranty flows.

Nguyen tac san pham:

- Khong luu password/cookie/ma 2FA trong he thong.
- Khong quang cao cong khai bang ngon ngu "unlock", "bao live", "bao khong die", "keo Zalo ne policy".
- Khong ban follower fake, engagement fake, tool tang tuong tac.
- Khong ho tro nganh cam/nhay cam hoac noi dung vi pham phap luat/chinh sach nen tang.
- Moi don hang can co risk disclosure va chinh sach bao hanh gioi han.
- He thong chi ghi nhan checklist ban giao, trang thai, bang chung so huu/kiem tra, khong thuc hien automation trai phep.

Suggested public wording:

- "Kenh TikTok starter da audit cho shop/doanh nghiep."
- "Kenh co tuong tac san, phu hop de bat dau lam content."
- "Ban giao kem report va playbook van hanh co ban."
- "Khong cam ket reach/don hang/tinh nang vinh vien vi phu thuoc nen tang va cach van hanh cua nguoi mua."

Kiem tra kenh theo checklist, cham diem chat luong, phan loai niche, de xuat gia ban va trang thai sellable/not sellable.

Quan ly content starter pack, gan task cho editor, duyet output va ban giao file cho khach.

### 9.6 Editor

Nhan brief co scope co dinh, san xuat video, upload version, nhan feedback gioi han va chot ban giao.

### 9.7 Customer / Buyer

Xem public listing hoac nhan listing qua sales, de lai nhu cau, dat coc, nhan ban giao kenh, nhan content pack neu mua kem.

## 10. Product Modules

### 10.1 Channel Inventory

Quan ly danh sach kenh TikTok trong kho.

Core fields:

- Channel ID noi bo.
- Platform.
- Username/handle.
- Display name.
- Follower count.
- Niche cu.
- Suggested buyer niche.
- Country/audience note.
- Average views last 10 videos.
- Average engagement.
- Live status.
- Link bio status.
- Violation status.
- Last active date.
- Sourcing cost.
- Suggested sale price.
- Minimum sale price.
- Inventory status.
- Risk score.
- Audit score.
- Internal notes.

Inventory statuses:

- Sourced.
- In audit.
- Needs review.
- Sellable.
- Reserved.
- Sold.
- In handover.
- Warranty active.
- Completed.
- Rejected.
- Archived.

### 10.2 Audit Engine

Cham diem va tao report kenh.

Audit categories:

- Ownership readiness.
- Follower authenticity signal.
- Recent activity.
- Engagement quality.
- Niche alignment.
- Violation/security risk.
- Feature readiness.
- Buyer suitability.

Score model:

- Audit score: 0-100.
- Risk level: low / medium / high.
- Sell recommendation: sell / hold / reject.
- Suggested price band.

Audit output:

- Internal report day du.
- Customer-facing report rut gon.
- Risk disclosure text.
- Checklist item pass/fail.

### 10.3 Public Catalog / Landing

Trang public khong nen hien toan bo thong tin nhay cam cua kenh. Muc tieu la tao lead va giup khach hieu loai hang.

Public listing can show:

- Channel tier.
- Follower range.
- Niche category.
- Engagement summary.
- Risk level summary.
- Price range hoac "lien he bao gia".
- Add-on content pack.
- CTA: nhan danh sach kenh / dat kenh theo nganh / tu van nhanh.

Public listing should not show by default:

- Full username neu chua dat coc.
- Sensitive ownership detail.
- Supplier/source.
- Internal scoring notes.

### 10.4 Lead And Reservation

Quan ly lead va quy trinh giu cho.

Lead fields:

- Name.
- Phone/Zalo.
- Business type.
- Product category.
- Target use case.
- Budget range.
- Need content pack: yes/no.
- Preferred channel tier.
- Source URL/campaign.
- Sales owner.
- Lead status.

Reservation rules:

- Kenh chi duoc giu cho khi sales tao reservation.
- Moi reservation co expiry time.
- Khi reserved, listing khong hien la available tren public catalog.
- Neu het han chua thanh toan, kenh quay ve sellable.

### 10.5 Order And Payment Tracking

MVP chi can ghi nhan thanh toan thu cong.

Order fields:

- Order code.
- Customer.
- Channel.
- Content pack add-on.
- Channel sale price.
- Content pack price.
- Discount.
- Deposit amount.
- Paid amount.
- Payment status.
- Payment proof.
- Sales owner.
- Order status.

Order statuses:

- Draft.
- Quoted.
- Deposit pending.
- Deposit paid.
- Full payment pending.
- Paid.
- In handover.
- Handover completed.
- Warranty active.
- Completed.
- Cancelled.
- Refunded.

### 10.6 Handover Workflow

Quy trinh ban giao can co checklist de giam tranh chap.

Checklist:

- Customer confirmed product/category.
- Risk disclosure accepted.
- Payment/deposit confirmed.
- Channel status rechecked before handover.
- Handover instructions sent.
- Customer confirms access.
- Customer updates recovery/security info.
- Final handover confirmation captured.
- Warranty start date recorded.

System requirement:

- Moi buoc co timestamp va actor.
- Co upload bang chung: screenshot, video, document.
- Co note neu co issue.
- Khong luu password/cookie/2FA trong he thong.

### 10.7 Warranty And Dispute

Bao hanh can gioi han theo chinh sach kinh doanh.

Recommended warranty scope:

- Bao hanh quyen truy cap trong 3-7 ngay neu loi xuat phat truoc ban giao.
- Khong bao hanh reach, don hang, live, link bio, TikTok Shop, ads, hoac tinh nang thay doi theo nen tang.
- Khong bao hanh neu khach doi thong tin sai, spam content, chay nganh nhay cam, vi pham policy, hoac giao account cho ben thu ba.

System requirement:

- Tao warranty case.
- Gan order/channel.
- Log ly do, bang chung, trang thai xu ly.
- Mark case outcome: valid / invalid / resolved / rejected / refunded / replaced.

### 10.8 Content Starter Pack

Add-on de tan dung doi editor nhung khong bien thanh full-service.

Suggested packs:

- Pack A: 5 video starter.
- Pack B: 10 video starter.
- Pack C: 20 video starter.
- Script-only pack: 20 hook/caption/CTA.
- Live starter pack: 3 kich ban live ngan + FAQ + CTA.

Rules:

- Scope co dinh.
- So lan chinh sua gioi han.
- Deadline ro rang.
- Khong bao gom van hanh dang bai/live neu khong co goi rieng.

Workflow:

```text
Order paid -> Content brief -> Editor assigned -> Version uploaded -> Review -> One revision -> Approved -> Delivered
```

### 10.9 Playbook Library

Tai lieu ban kem hoac gui tu dong sau khi ban giao.

Playbook examples:

- Checklist doi profile sau khi nhan kenh.
- 7 ngay dau nen lam gi.
- 20 idea content keo lead.
- CTA mem ve Zalo/link bio.
- Nhung loi de bi giam reach.
- Huong dan briefing editor.
- Mau landing/link bio.

### 10.10 Analytics Dashboard

Dashboard cho founder/admin:

- Doanh thu theo thang.
- Loi nhuan theo kenh.
- Inventory sell-through.
- Best-selling niche.
- Average margin by source.
- Content pack attach rate.
- Warranty/dispute rate.
- Sales conversion funnel.

2. Customer chap nhan risk disclosure.

## 18. Key Risks

### 18.1 Platform Policy Risk

Rui ro: nen tang thay doi policy, han che tinh nang, khoa tai khoan, hoac khong cho chuyen giao.

Mitigation:

- Khong cam ket tinh nang vinh vien.
- Bat buoc co risk disclosure.
- Audit lich su vi pham.
- Khong luu credential.
- Khong quang cao bang ngon ngu vi pham.

### 18.2 Inventory Quality Risk

Rui ro: kenh co follower ao, tuong tac chet, niche lech, bi ep gia.

Mitigation:

- Audit checklist.
- Risk score.
- Customer-facing summary.
- Gia theo tier, khong theo follower count don thuan.

### 18.3 Dispute Risk

Rui ro: khach doi lai tien vi reach thap, khong ra don, mat tinh nang.

Mitigation:

- Chinh sach bao hanh gioi han.
- Handover checklist.
- Payment/order proof.
- Warranty case decision log.

### 18.4 Founder Bottleneck

Rui ro: founder phai tu van/ban giao/support moi don.

Mitigation:

- Sales script.
- Playbook.
- Standard package.
- Handover checklist.
- Fixed-scope content pack.

### 18.5 Editor Scope Creep

Rui ro: content pack bien thanh dich vu sua vo han.

Mitigation:

- Revision limit.
- Brief template.
- Delivery criteria.
- Extra revision fee.
- No monthly operation in default package.

## 19. Tech Architecture Notes

- Build manual-first, API-ready.
- Use modular domain boundaries: inventory, audit, lead, order, handover, warranty, content_pack.
- Add audit log for every status change.
- Keep sensitive account transfer details out of persistent storage.
- Use role-based permission from MVP.
- Public catalog should use anonymized data and not leak full handle/source by default.
- Every business entity should support internal notes and customer-facing notes separately.
- Prefer status machine over free-form status text.
- Use file upload/storage for evidence/payment proof/content delivery.
